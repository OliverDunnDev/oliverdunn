01 / About

Curious by default.
Practical by design.

I move between software engineering, data and research. The common thread is working out how things behave, then building something reliable around that understanding.

I graduated from the University of Hull with First Class Honours in Computer Science. My first professional engineering role was at Tribal Group, working on SITS, an established higher-education platform used by universities internationally.

That meant working across JavaScript, Uniface and SQL, tracing issues through unfamiliar application layers, delivering features and dealing with the realities of maintaining long-lived software. One of my larger pieces of work involved independently resolving 86 of 123 identified security items in two weeks.

Alongside engineering, I've worked on Python data projects and published undergraduate research into safety assessment for quantum machine-learning classifiers. Away from the conventional stack, I enjoy creative coding, audio and film-related projects.
